SMS Terms

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Program

Manzo Capital LLC, operating as IBuyRedding, sends non-marketing text messages about property inquiries to people who opt in on our website. Messages include follow-ups about your inquiry and appointment confirmations. We do not send promotional messages through this program.

How to opt in

You opt in by ticking the optional SMS box when you submit an inquiry on our website. Consent is not required to submit an inquiry or use our services.

How to opt out (STOP)

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or call us at (530) 638-2296.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your inquiry and any appointments you schedule.

Charges

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan. IBuyRedding does not charge for text messages.

Carriers and delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier and device, and is not guaranteed.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
