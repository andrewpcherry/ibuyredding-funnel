Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

IBuyRedding is a brand owned and operated by Manzo Capital LLC (“IBuyRedding”, “we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you complete our property questionnaire or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, and your answers about the property and your situation.
  • Your text message choice. If you tick the optional SMS box, we record that you consented, when, and the page you consented on. If you reply STOP, we record that too.
  • Website usage. Like most websites, we may collect browser type, IP address and pages visited to understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

  • To review your property, respond to your inquiry, and prepare and discuss an offer.
  • To call you, and, only if you consented, to send non-marketing text messages about your inquiry, including follow-ups and appointment confirmations.
  • To keep records of our conversations and transactions, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not send promotional text messages unless you have given separate consent for them.

Mobile numbers and SMS consent

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or your SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent records are never shared with any third party, except with the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf and are not permitted to use them for anything else.

See our SMS Terms for how our text messages work, including how to opt out.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer relationship management system, phone and text messaging providers, and website hosting, who may use it only to provide those services to us;
  • With title, escrow and other professionals needed to complete a transaction you choose to move forward with; and
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Keeping your information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction, and limit access to the people who need it to respond to you.

Cookies and tracking

The embedded HighLevel inquiry form may use cookies or browser storage to operate. Your browser lets you manage cookies. Blocking them may affect the form. You can also call us to submit an inquiry.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Call us at (530) 638-2296 to ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it. California residents may have additional rights under California law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
